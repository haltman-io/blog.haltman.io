This is a small static blog for notes, experiments, and practical writing about software.

The project is intentionally simple. Posts and pages are stored as MDX files, the build is static, and the output can be deployed to any CDN.

## What appears here

Expect short essays, implementation notes, interface observations, and references that are useful enough to keep around.

## How the site works

There is no backend or CMS. The repository is the source of truth, and every public page comes from a file.
