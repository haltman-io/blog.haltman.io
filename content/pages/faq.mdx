## Can pages be hidden from the navbar and footer?

Yes. Set both `navbar.show` and `footer.show` to `false`. The page still builds and remains accessible by URL.

## Can the footer link to external pages?

Yes. Add a link under `footer.links` in `blog-settings.json`. You do not need to create an MDX page for direct external links.

## Can pages use nested slugs?

Yes. Put the file in a nested folder under `content/pages` or set a custom `slug` in front matter.

## Do pages appear in the RSS feed?

No. RSS includes posts only. Pages are included in the sitemap.
